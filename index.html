Sustainable Design

Eco-Engineered Construction

MAIA The Seven employs a technologically advanced construction methodology that blends digital accuracy with sustainable execution. This system minimizes unnecessary material use while enhancing structural integrity at every stage. From initial planning to final detailing, precision engineering drives better resource management, long-term durability, and environmentally responsible development. The result is a structure that is both future-ready and deeply aligned with eco-conscious building principles.

Signature Architecture

Twin Towers, One Sky Bridge

Two landmark residential towers are joined by sculptural, landscaped sky bridges that create a distinctive silhouette on the South Bengaluru skyline. The bridges double as elevated lounges and garden decks, giving residents a shared vantage point over the tree-lined streets of Basavanagudi while keeping each wing private and self-contained.

Spatial Clarity

Three-Side-Open, No Common Walls

Every residence is planned with more than three open sides and no shared walls with a neighbour, so light, air and views flow through the home uninterrupted. Expansive 4,400–4,950 sq.ft. layouts with generous decks bring the openness of a bungalow to a secure, high-rise address.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Maia the seven different from other luxury apartments in Bangalore?
Maia the seven stands apart due to its ultra-low density design, with only 128 exclusive 4 BHK residences across 36 floors. Each floor has just two apartments, one per wing, ensuring unmatched privacy, abundant natural light, and a villa-like living experience in the heart of Basavanagudi.
Is Maia the seven a good choice for large families?
Yes. Every home is a spacious 4 BHK ranging from 4,400 to 4,950 sq.ft., with generous private decks, separate family and formal living zones, and staff quarters — ideal for multi-generational families who value both togetherness and privacy.
What configurations are available at Maia the seven?
Maia the seven is a single-typology community offering only 4 BHK residences in two variants — Luxury Type A (4,400 sq.ft.) and Ultra-Luxury Type B (4,950 sq.ft.) — starting from ₹12.5 Cr*.
Where is Maia the seven located?
The project is located in Basavanagudi, South Bengaluru — minutes from Bull Temple, Gandhi Bazaar, Lalbagh and the Jayanagar and South End Circle metro stations.
What kind of amenities are available at Maia the seven?
Residents enjoy infinity and sky pools, a Technogym-equipped fitness centre, gourmet dining with a dedicated chef, yoga and zen decks, a private 4K theatre and gaming lounge, 5-tier smart security and MAIA Life concierge services, spread across 100,000+ sq.ft. of amenity space.
How does Maia the seven ensure privacy for residents?
With just two homes per floor, no common walls between residences, private lift lobbies and over 72% open landscaped area, every home feels like an independent bungalow in the sky.
Does Maia the seven offer good metro connectivity?
Yes. Jayanagar Metro Station is about 2.3 km away and Rashtreeya Vidyalaya Road Metro about 2.8 km, both on the Green Line, giving quick access to MG Road, Majestic and the rest of the city.
Is Maia the seven suitable as a long-term investment?
Basavanagudi is a land-constrained heritage micro-market where new launches are rare. A 3.7-acre, 128-home development here is virtually unprecedented, making it a scarce, legacy-grade asset with strong long-term appreciation potential.
What type of buyers is Maia the seven designed for?
It is designed for discerning end-users and HNI investors looking for a bungalow-substitute in central South Bengaluru — those who value heritage surroundings, low density and hotel-inspired living.
Why is Maia the seven considered a landmark project in Basavanagudi?
Its scale, twin sky-bridge towers, flowing architectural signature and single-format 4 BHK inventory make it the largest ultra-luxury development the neighbourhood has seen in over a decade.
